Good to Know

Questions & Answers

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 5:30 pm. The event starts at 6 pm sharp, and late arrivals may not be let in until the ceremony is over.

Is the event indoors or outdoors?

The event is indoors, with an outdoor terrace.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Plus-ones must be confirmed by Alex or Kaila.

Are children invited?

All children must be confirmed by Alex or Kaila.

What should I wear?

Elegant evening attire. Floor-length or midi dresses, dressy jumpsuits, and suits are welcome. Please avoid white, ivory, and deep wine; these colors are reserved for the wedding party.

Where can I park?

The hotel offers all-day parking for $20. In and out is fine.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Yes, photos and videos with no flash are fine. Please do not get in the way of our photographers and videographers.
